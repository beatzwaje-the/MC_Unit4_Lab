==============================================================================
RECOMMENDATION
==============================================================================
LoRaWAN (SF12) with one privately owned gateway.
  * Link: MCL 151 dB (1 dB below NB-IoT), +27.9 dB margin at 8 km, still +18.9 dB at n = 4.5.
  * Battery: ~0.02 mAh/cycle -> decades on 19 Ah, versus ~5 y (NB-IoT, marginal) and ~3.5 y (LTE-M, fails).
  * Section 4.3 axes: no cellular coverage nearby rules out carrier-based NB-IoT/LTE-M/RedCap regardless of
    margin; own gateway = no per-device subscription at 200 units; low data rate and stationary nodes suit LoRa.
